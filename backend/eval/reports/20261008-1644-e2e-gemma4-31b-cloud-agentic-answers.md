## M01 (multi_hop): It is 5pm on Friday and ransomware has hit our hospital, which is an NCII entity. What are we legally required to do, and by when?

**Status:** answered

**Given to the writer:** pua220:r2:1 pua220:r1:1 pua220:r3:1 act854:s23:1 act854:s16:1 act854:long_title:1 act854:s35:1 · **Cited:** pua220:r2:1 act854:s23:1 pua220:r3:1

You must immediately notify the Chief Executive and your national critical information infrastructure sector lead by electronic means [1], [2].

* Within six hours of the incident coming to your knowledge, you must submit particulars of the authorized person, the NCII entity, the NCII sector, the NCII sector lead, and information on the incident (type, description, severity, known date/time of occurrence, and method of discovery) [1].
* Within fourteen days after the initial notification, you must provide supplementary information to the fullest extent practicable, including affected NCII particulars, estimated number of affected hosts, threat actor particulars, artifacts, related incidents, tactics, techniques and procedures, impact on the NCII or computer systems, and actions taken [1].
* Information for the six-hour and fourteen-day requirements must be submitted through the National Cyber Coordination and Command Centre System, or via communication determined by the Chief Executive if that system is disrupted [3].
* You must provide further updates on the incident from time to time as required by the Chief Executive [1].

**Key facts expected:**
- Notify the Chief Executive and the NCII sector lead (s23(1))
- Immediately by electronic means; initial particulars within six hours; supplementary information within fourteen days (PUA220 reg 2)
- Through the National Cyber Coordination and Command Centre System (PUA220 reg 3)
- Failing to notify: fine not exceeding RM500,000 or imprisonment not exceeding ten years or both (s23(2))
